Option Notes

Compact Status Bar is the closest replacement for the current section. It is clean, minimal, and keeps the route map separate.

Map Control Panel merges the locate status into the map area. It feels more immersive but gives less text room on small screens.

Driver GPS Console is best if drivers need an operational GPS readout. It is the most explicit and the largest option.
